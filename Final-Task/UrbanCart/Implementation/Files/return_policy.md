# UrbanCart Return Policy
Document type: returns
Version: 3.0
Effective date: 1 August 2026
Owner: Operations

## Eligibility window
Unused products may be returned within 7 days of delivery. The 7-day window starts on the day the courier marks the parcel as delivered.

Headphones, earphones, wearables and other personal-care electronics follow the same 7-day unused-and-sealed rule.

A return requested after 7 days is outside policy and cannot be approved by the AI receptionist. Support may review an exception only for manufacturing defects discovered after the window.

## Condition of goods
Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all tags, chargers, manuals and free gifts. Opened hygiene or ear-tip products are not eligible unless defective on arrival.

## How to start a return
Contact UrbanCart with the order code (for example UC-10452), product name and reason. Support will issue a Return Merchandise Authorisation (RMA) if the request is inside policy.

## Refund timing
Approved returns are refunded within 7–10 business days after the warehouse receives and inspects the item. Original payment method or bank transfer is used. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer.

## What this policy does not cover
- Change of mind after 7 days
- Damage caused by the customer
- Missing accessories that were confirmed delivered
- Products marked “Final Sale”

Damaged-on-arrival cases are handled under the Support Guidelines, not as a standard return.
