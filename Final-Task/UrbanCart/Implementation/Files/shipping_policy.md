# UrbanCart Shipping Policy
Document type: shipping
Version: 2.1
Effective date: 15 July 2026
Owner: Operations

## Coverage
UrbanCart delivers across Pakistan. Major cities with standard coverage: Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, Sialkot, Gujranwala and Hyderabad.

We deliver to Lahore. Lahore is a primary service city.

## Delivery times
Estimated delivery after dispatch:
- Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi: 1–2 business days
- Other major cities listed above: 2–4 business days
- Smaller cities and towns: 3–5 business days
- Remote or mountain areas: 5–8 business days, confirmed before dispatch

These are estimates, not guarantees. Public holidays and courier disruptions can add time.

## Shipping charges
Orders above Rs. 5,000 to Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad usually qualify for free standard shipping. Below that threshold a city-based courier fee is added at checkout.

## Cash on delivery
COD is available in covered cities. The customer should keep the exact order amount ready. Failed COD attempts may delay the next delivery slot.

## What we do not promise automatically
The receptionist must not invent a same-day promise. Same-day Lahore delivery exists only when the product page shows “Same-day eligible” and the order is placed before 12:00.
