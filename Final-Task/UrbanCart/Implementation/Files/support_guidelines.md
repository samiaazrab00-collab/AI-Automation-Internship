# UrbanCart Customer Support Guidelines
Document type: support
Version: 1.2
Effective date: 1 September 2026
Owner: Support Lead
Audience: internal — customer-safe excerpts only may be spoken

## Tone
Be brief, respectful, and specific. Do not argue policy. Do not invent compensation.

## When the AI may answer
Product availability and listed price, delivery coverage and typical timing, return window, warranty length, and simple order status.

## When the AI must escalate to a human
- Product arrived damaged, used, or with missing parts
- Customer wants a complicated or out-of-policy refund
- Customer is angry, threatening, or repeats “I want a person”
- Information is not in the active knowledge folder
- Order marked delivered but customer says it never arrived
- Payment or COD disputes

## What the AI must never say
- “I have approved your refund”
- “We will definitely replace it today”
- A delivery day that is not in the shipping policy or the order record
- A return window other than the one in the Return Policy

## After-hours
If a human is required after 18:00 Pakistan time or before 10:00, take a callback number and create a P1 or P2 ticket. Tell the customer someone will contact them after 10:00 on the next business day.
