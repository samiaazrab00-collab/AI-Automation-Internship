# UrbanCart Warranty Information
Document type: warranty
Version: 1.4
Effective date: 1 June 2026
Owner: After-sales

## Standard warranty
- Branded electronics (phones, laptops, tablets): 12 months manufacturer warranty unless the product card says otherwise
- Audio accessories including headphones and earbuds: 6 months warranty against manufacturing defects
- Home appliances: 12 months on the unit, 6 months on accessories
- Lifestyle and non-electronic goods: 15-day manufacturing-defect cover only

## What warranty covers
Defects in materials and workmanship during normal use. Warranty service is provided through UrbanCart support, who will coordinate with the brand service centre where required.

## What warranty does not cover
Physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorised repair, missing serial numbers, and consumables (ear tips, cables sold separately).

## Claiming warranty
Share the order code, product serial/IMEI if applicable, and a short description of the fault. Support will log a warranty ticket. Warranty is not the same as the 7-day return window.
